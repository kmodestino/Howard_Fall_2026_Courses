Instead of class today, I am asking students to watch one of three versions of Hamlet. Because we will have one more day on Hamlet next week, and I know people still need participation points for Week 9 and 10, I will extend this "two week" unit to include next Tuesday's class. Please come prepared with some ideas about Hamlet (in particular how the play stages Art as an alternative to and compensation for Justice).

Pick one version below and watching it. These are all YouTube Links, so they will be interrupted by commercials. It may be worth your time seeing if you have access to one of these versions on a streaming service you can access to save yourself the commercials. 

1: [Kenneth Branagh](https://youtu.be/1vIrIreGHVI?si=jRpjMQvyjuHgGngy) This is great, but it is the complete text and thus four hours long. Highly recommended if you have the time. 

2: [Mel Gibson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lJZYIv1AiKs) Personally, I don't like this version, but aside from Gibson, there are some good performances and its much shorter.

3: [Peter Brooks/Adrian Lester](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=84bAaCjSlLA) Filmed version of a stage play from the Théâtres de Bouffes du Nord in Paris (still in English). Highly acclaimed staging, much more diverse cast than the previous two.
