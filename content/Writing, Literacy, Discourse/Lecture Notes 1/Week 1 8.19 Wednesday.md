## Close Reading Framework

Five-step model introduced using "The Red Wheelbarrow" as a shared example: 14

- Scene setting — establish context and common ground for the reader
- Noticing — identify something strange, surprising, or unexpected in the text 15
- Local claiming — make a debatable claim about the specific detail noticed 1617
- Regional argumentation — extend the claim to explain how the whole text works 18
- Global theorizing — connect the reading to broader ideas beyond the text itself 19

Key point: a claim must be disputable; pure observation is not yet argument. 17
