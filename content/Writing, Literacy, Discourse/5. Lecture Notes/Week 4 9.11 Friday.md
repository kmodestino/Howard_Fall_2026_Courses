## Key Outcomes

Students were introduced to the claim-data-warrant structural model for arguments and assigned a two-part writing exercise to analyze and apply these concepts. 1 The assignment is due Sunday, September 13th at midnight. 1

## Argument Structure Definitions

- Claim: An arguable statement or "hot take" that the writer wants the reader to accept; it cannot be a simple fact. 2
- Data: The proof, logic, examples, or facts used to support the claim. 2
- Warrant: The underlying assumption that connects the data to the claim; it is the reasoning move that makes the data relevant. 23
- Vulnerability: Warrants are often the weakest point of an argument because they are frequently unstated or assumed to be self-evident, allowing opponents to challenge the logic. 4

## Assignment Requirements

- Analysis Task: Select one paragraph from Common Sense or one grievance from the Declaration of Independence and identify its claim, data, and warrant in one sentence each. 4
- Original Writing Task: Write an original paragraph on any topic of interest that includes a clear claim, data, and warrant. 4
- Formatting: Students must clearly identify or underline the three components (e.g., using a color scheme). 4
- Expectations: Students are encouraged to explore their own writing voice and avoid doing the bare minimum. 56
