## Key Outcomes

Students were granted the remainder of the class period to complete their thesis statement assignments due to low submission rates 12. The instructor introduced a new focus on the rhetorical integration of evidence, moving beyond logical warrants to examine how writers maintain authority when using long quotes or paraphrases 23.

## Instructional Focus: Evidence Integration

- Objective: Shift from analyzing the "unstated warrant" to studying the rhetorical methods used to introduce and reintegrate evidence 2.
- Goal: Prevent long examples or quotations from displacing the writer's own voice and authority 3.
- Methodology: Move beyond the basic "sandwich" method (introduce, quote, explain) to analyze techniques used by professional writers 3.
- Case Study: Students are assigned to read Chapter 10 of Frederick Douglass's narrative, specifically focusing on the fight with Covey, to analyze how Douglass integrates extensive evidence into his personal story 45.
