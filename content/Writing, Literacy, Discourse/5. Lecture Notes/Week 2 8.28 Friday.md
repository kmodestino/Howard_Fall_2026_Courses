## Stylistic Definitions

- Parataxis: Places clauses side-by-side as equals using coordinating conjunctions (and, but, or) or punctuation (commas, semicolons) 1. It registers events in the order perceived without asserting explicit logic 9.
- Hypotaxis: Ranks clauses by making one independent and others dependent using subordinating conjunctions (because, although, since, while, if, when, that) or relative pronouns 2. It makes logical relations (cause, purpose, condition) explicit 2.
