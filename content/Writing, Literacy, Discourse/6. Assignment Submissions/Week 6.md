# Week 6 — Claim and Support

**Due:** choose one of your thesis statements, attempt to re-write it as a paragraph topic, offering it as a claim with a paragraph of supporting evidence

**Instructions**

- Take your strongest thesis from Week 5 and revise it if needed to fit the scope of a paragraph
- Write it as the first sentence of a paragraph.
- Add 5–7 more sentences that support the claim using specific evidence from your sources. 
- You do not need to integrate any quotations (we will work on this next week) just general statements of evidence and paraphrase
- This is unlikely to work as a paragraph in your final essay. It is simply a way to test if your thesis offers itself to more writing and analysis, if you can gather the type of evidence you will need. Your final essay will probably involve significantly revising the thesis statement AND individual paragraphs will be shaped by subordinate pieces of the overall argument, not the entire thesis.
