**Course Books**

1. **Homer.** _The Odyssey_. Translated by Emily Wilson. New York: W. W. Norton & Company, 2018. (Weeks 1–4)
    
2. **_The Epic of Gilgamesh_**. Translated by Andrew George. London: Penguin Classics, 2003. (Week 5)
    
3. **Niane, Djibril Tamsir.** _Sundiata: An Epic of Old Mali_. Translated by G. D. Pickett. Revised edition. London: Longman, 2006. (Week 6)
    
4. **Andrade, Mário de.** _Macunaíma_. Translated by E. A. Goodland. New York: Random House, 1984. (Weeks 7–8)
    
5. **Dangarembga, Tsitsi.** _Nervous Conditions_. London: Women's Press, 1988. (Weeks 9–10)
    
6. **Desai, Kiran.** _The Inheritance of Loss_. New York: Atlantic Monthly Press, 2006. (Weeks 10–13)
    
7. **Hamid, Mohsin.** _Exit West_. New York: Riverhead Books, 2017. (Weeks 13–14)
